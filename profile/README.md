## DailyAds.pk

[DailyAds.pk](https://dailyads.pk) collects the advertisements printed every day
in Pakistan's newspapers — government and private jobs, tenders, admissions,
public and court notices, auctions and results — from Dawn, Jang, Express,
The News, Nawa-i-Waqt, Dunya, The Nation, The Express Tribune and more, and
makes them searchable in one place.

### Open source

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [dailyads-android](https://github.com/dailyads-pk/dailyads-android) | The Android app — Flutter, GPL-3.0. No account, no ads, no tracking. |

### For developers

Every public page on dailyads.pk is also available as markdown with
`Accept: text/markdown`, there is an [RSS feed](https://dailyads.pk/rss.xml)
of new advertisements, and [llms.txt](https://dailyads.pk/llms.txt) describes
the site for AI agents. Archive figures are published on the
[statistics page](https://dailyads.pk/statistics).

DailyAds.pk is an independent archive, not affiliated with any government
department, advertiser or newspaper.
